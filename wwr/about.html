THE RIVER'S LODGE

At The River's Edge, we believe life is better with a little adventure. Our purpose is to bring people closer to nature and one another through exciting river experiences, creating unforgettable memories along the way.Through adventure, teamwork and a commitment to protecting nature, we aim to inspire confidence, encourage exploration and create experiences worth remembering.

History

The River's Edge began with a young girl who grew up finding happiness in the pleasures of playing by the river.To me, the water was and is a place of freedom, adventure and endless childhood memories.As life took me elsewhere, I found myself missing the river and the connection I once with it. One day, while visiting the beautiful Modderfontein Reserve I came across a flowing river.

Watching the water sparked an idea: what if I could recreate that feeling of happiness, nostalgia and belonging for others? And so, The River's Edge was born, a rafting company inspired by childhood memories, a love for nature and the belief that everyone deserves a little adventure. Because where the river leads, adventure follows.
